About Mrspeechie

Therapy built around one clinician, one client, one goal at a time

Mrspeechie was founded on a simple idea: progress in speech therapy comes from consistency and trust, not caseloads. Every client sees the same clinician, session after session.

Meet your clinician

Dr. Anjali Menon

Anjali is a certified speech-language pathologist who has spent over twelve years working across paediatric clinics, hospital rehabilitation units, and private practice. She founded Mrspeechie to give clients the kind of unhurried, one-on-one attention that's hard to find in larger clinics.

Her approach blends structured clinical technique with genuine warmth — she believes a client who feels at ease makes faster progress than one who feels tested.

M.Sc. Speech-Language Pathology RCI Licensed Certified Fluency Specialist 12+ years practice

What guides a session

Three things every client can expect

1

No rushing

Sessions are 45–60 minutes, and the plan adjusts to how each person actually progresses — not a fixed curriculum.

2

Plain explanations

You'll always know why an exercise is being used and what it's building toward — no jargon left unexplained.

3

Practice that fits real life

Home exercises are short and specific, designed to fit into an evening rather than take one over.
