About Mr. Speechie

Better Understanding. Better Support. Better Communication.

Mr. Speechie aims to make Speech-Language Pathology more accessible, understandable and evidence-informed — through personalised therapy, online and offline support, reliable information, and a community that connects individuals, parents, caregivers, students and professionals.

Meet your clinician

Muhammed Swalih N. A.

Muhammed Swalih N. A. is a Speech-Language Pathologist with professional training from the All India Institute of Speech and Hearing (AIISH), Mysore. He works with children and adults experiencing difficulties related to speech, language, communication, voice, fluency and swallowing.

His approach focuses on understanding each individual's strengths, needs and communication goals, followed by personalised assessment and therapy.

He is currently working as Speech and Swallow Therapist at Shifaa Hospital, Bangalore, and has previously worked as Lecturer in Audiology and Speech Pathology at ICCONS, Shornur.

Through Mr. Speechie, his aim is to make reliable and easy-to-understand information about speech, language, voice, fluency and swallowing accessible to parents, caregivers, individuals, students and professionals.

What guides a session

Three things every client can expect

1

No rushing

Sessions are 45–60 minutes, and the plan adjusts to how each person actually progresses — not a fixed curriculum.

2

Plain explanations

You'll always know why an exercise is being used and what it's building toward — no jargon left unexplained.

3

Practice that fits real life

Home exercises are short and specific, designed to fit into an evening rather than take one over.
