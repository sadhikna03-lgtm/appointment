How sessions work

Four steps from first call to steady progress

01

Book an evaluation

A 45-minute session to understand the concern and where things currently stand.

02

Get a therapy plan

Clear goals, a session cadence, and what to expect over the next few months.

03

Weekly sessions

Focused, one-on-one practice — in clinic or online — with homework that fits real life.

04

Progress reviews

Every six weeks, we check what's working and adjust the plan together.
