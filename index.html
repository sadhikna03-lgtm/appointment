How Therapy Works

From Understanding the Concern to Meaningful Progress.

01

Initial Consultation & Assessment

We begin by understanding the individual's concerns, history, communication needs, strengths and goals. Detailed assessment is conducted when required.

02

Individualised Therapy Plan

Based on assessment findings and individual goals, appropriate intervention strategies are planned according to the person's needs and priorities.

03

Therapy & Home Support

Therapy focuses on meaningful and functional goals through structured activities and personalised strategies. Parent or caregiver guidance is provided when appropriate.

04

Progress Monitoring

Progress is monitored regularly, and goals or intervention strategies are modified according to the individual's response, changing needs and functional progress.
