About Therapy

UNDERSTANDING SPEECH, LANGUAGE & LEARNING

Communication, Learning and Participation Go Hand in Hand.

Speech-Language Therapy supports individuals experiencing difficulties with speech, language, communication, learning, academic communication, voice, fluency, cognitive-communication and swallowing.

Speech and language skills play an important role in how children understand, learn, read, write, express ideas and participate in academic activities.

A Speech-Language Pathologist assesses an individual's communication and related learning needs and develops an appropriate intervention plan based on their strengths, challenges and goals.

Therapy may support a child with speech or language difficulties, a child experiencing language-based learning or academic communication difficulties, an adult recovering from a neurological condition, someone experiencing voice or fluency difficulties, or an individual with swallowing or feeding concerns.

The goal is not just better speech — it is better communication, learning and meaningful participation in everyday life.
