Services

Six areas of practice, one therapist for each client

Every service below starts with a short evaluation so the plan matches what's actually going on — not a generic programme.

Ar

Articulation therapy

For children and adults who have trouble producing specific sounds clearly — like R, S, L, or "th". Sessions use targeted drills and everyday speech practice.

Ages 3+
La

Language delay

Support for children who are behind on vocabulary, sentence structure, or understanding spoken language, built through play-based, everyday interaction.

Ages 2–10
St

Stuttering & fluency

Techniques to build smoother, more confident speech, plus strategies for the moments stuttering feels hardest — presentations, calls, introductions.

All ages
Vo

Voice therapy

For vocal strain, hoarseness, or professional voice use — teachers, singers, and callers — focused on breath support and healthy vocal habits.

Teens & adults
Ap

Aphasia & stroke recovery

Structured rehabilitation for adults recovering speech and language after stroke or brain injury, coordinated with neurologists and physiotherapists.

Adults
So

Social communication

Support for conversation skills, turn-taking, and reading social cues — often useful alongside a broader developmental support plan.

Ages 4+
